Supporting Healthy Gums

Common gum conditions such as gingivitis and periodontitis can affect the health of your gums and the supporting structures of your teeth. We assess the condition and recommend appropriate treatment to help manage it.

  • Assessment of gum health and gum pockets
  • Deep cleaning to remove plaque and tartar below the gumline
  • Help reduce bleeding and inflammation
  • Ongoing maintenance and hygiene advice
