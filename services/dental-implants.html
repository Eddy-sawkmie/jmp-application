DENTAL IMPLANTS

Replacing a Missing Tooth with an Implant

A dental implant is a small post that is surgically placed in the jawbone. It can act as a stable foundation for a replacement tooth.

For a single missing tooth, a customized crown can be attached to the implant. For multiple missing teeth, implants may support a bridge or another restoration.

  • Provides a foundation for replacement teeth
  • Can support a crown or bridge
  • Designed to function with the surrounding teeth
  • Treatment is planned according to individual needs
WHY CONSIDER IMPLANTS

What Dental Implants Can Help With

Dental implants can be one option for replacing missing teeth. Your dentist can discuss whether they are suitable for your situation.

Single Missing Tooth

An implant can provide support for a replacement crown when a single tooth is missing.

Multiple Missing Teeth

Multiple implants may be used to support restorations such as an implant-supported bridge.

Individual Treatment

Implant treatment requires an examination and treatment plan based on your oral health and needs.

TREATMENT PROCESS

What to Expect

The exact sequence and timing can vary depending on your individual treatment plan.

1
Consultation

Examination and planning to determine suitable treatment options.

2
Implant Placement

The implant is surgically placed in the jawbone when planned by the dentist.

3
Healing

The bone can heal around the implant during the integration process.

4
Final Restoration

A crown, bridge or other restoration may then be attached according to the treatment plan.
