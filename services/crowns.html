DENTAL CROWNS

Strengthen and Restore Damaged Teeth

A dental crown is a restoration that covers a tooth to help restore its shape, strength and appearance.

Crowns may be considered for teeth that are damaged, weakened or have undergone certain dental treatments.

Your dentist can examine your teeth and determine whether a crown is appropriate for your individual situation.

CROWN TREATMENT

When a Crown May Be Considered

Damaged Teeth

Crowns can help restore teeth that have been significantly damaged or weakened.

Treated Teeth

A crown may be used to restore a tooth following certain dental treatments.

Tooth Appearance

Crowns can also restore the shape and appearance of a damaged tooth.

A Customized Treatment Plan

Every patient's dental condition is different. The type of restoration and treatment approach will depend on the condition of the tooth.

During your consultation, the dental team can evaluate the tooth and discuss the available restoration options with you.

Dental Evaluation

Examination of the tooth and surrounding oral structures.

Treatment Planning

A restoration plan based on your individual needs.

Final Restoration

Placement of the planned crown restoration.

TREATMENT PROCESS

What to Expect

The exact treatment process depends on your dental condition and the type of crown recommended.

1

Consultation

Your dental condition is evaluated and suitable crown options are discussed.

2

Tooth Preparation

The tooth is prepared and impressions are taken for a precise fit.

3

Crown Placement

The custom-made crown is carefully fitted and secured to the tooth.
