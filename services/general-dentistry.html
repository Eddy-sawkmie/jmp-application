Specialized Treatments

DENTURES

Complete & Partial Dentures

Dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth and surrounding tissues. We offer both complete dentures for patients missing all teeth and partial dentures for those missing some teeth. Our custom-made dentures restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

  • Complete dentures for full arch replacement
  • Partial dentures for multiple missing teeth
  • Custom-fit for comfort and function
  • Natural-looking appearance
SEDATION DENTISTRY

Anxiety-Free Dentistry

We understand that dental anxiety is common. Our sedation dentistry options help you feel relaxed and comfortable during your treatment. From mild sedation to deeper relaxation techniques, we ensure your dental experience is stress-free and pleasant.

  • Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for mild relaxation
  • Oral sedation for moderate anxiety
  • Safe and monitored procedures
  • Comfortable, pain-free experience
ORAL SURGERY

Bone Grafting

Bone grafting is a surgical procedure that rebuilds or regenerates bone in areas where it has been lost. This is often necessary before dental implant placement to ensure there is sufficient bone structure to support the implant. We use advanced techniques for optimal healing and integration.

  • Prepares jawbone for dental implants
  • Restores bone after tooth loss
  • Advanced grafting materials
  • Improved implant success rates
PERIODONTICS

Periodontal Flap Surgery

Periodontal flap surgery is an advanced treatment for periodontal disease. When gum disease has caused deep pockets that cannot be cleaned with scaling and root planing alone, this procedure allows us to access and thoroughly clean the tooth roots, promoting gum reattachment and healing.

  • Treats advanced periodontal disease
  • Reduces deep gum pockets
  • Promotes gum tissue regeneration
  • Prevents tooth loss
PERIODONTICS

Gum Grafting

Gum recession can expose tooth roots, causing sensitivity and increasing the risk of decay. Gum grafting surgery restores receded gum tissue by taking gum tissue from another area of your mouth or using donor tissue. This protects your teeth and improves the appearance of your smile.

  • Covers exposed tooth roots
  • Reduces tooth sensitivity
  • Prevents further gum recession
  • Improves smile aesthetics

PREVENTIVE DENTISTRY

Prevention Starts With Regular Care

Preventive dentistry focuses on keeping your teeth and gums healthy before problems develop. A few simple habits and routine visits can go a long way in protecting your smile:

  • Regular dental examinations
  • Professional cleaning
  • Good brushing and flossing habits
  • Limiting excessive sugary foods and drinks
  • Early detection of dental problems
