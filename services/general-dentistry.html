PREVENTIVE DENTISTRY

Prevention Starts With Regular Care

Preventive dentistry focuses on keeping your teeth and gums healthy before problems develop. A few simple habits and routine visits can go a long way in protecting your smile:

  • Regular dental examinations
  • Professional cleaning
  • Good brushing and flossing habits
  • Limiting excessive sugary foods and drinks
  • Early detection of dental problems
