Keeping Track of Your Oral Health

Regular dental examinations allow your dentist to monitor your teeth, gums, and overall oral health over time. This helps identify concerns such as tooth decay, gum disease, or other issues while they are still easier to manage.

  • Examination of teeth, gums, and oral tissues
  • Assessment of existing fillings and restorations
  • Personalised advice for your oral hygiene routine
  • Recommendations for a suitable check-up schedule
